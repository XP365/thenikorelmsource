FAQ

Q: How much do I need to pay?
A: It depends! Depending on how small it is, I might do it for free! For a smaller mod such as NG+, it would range from free to $20. For a larger mod such as 8 Modders, No Communication, that would run you about $50. However these prices are unfixed.

Q: How do you take payment?
A: Kofi. After a price is chosen I will open a commission on my account due to the variable price.

Q: How long do I need to wait?
A: Thats a tricky question to answer, since the complexity of the mod can make it varry from a day to a few months. Smaller mods like NG+ can be done in a few days, while 8 Modders, No Communication Pt 2 took me over a month to get my part working. I can give you a more accurate estimate once you tell me what it is you want!

Q: What all are you able to mod?
A: I specialize in Undertale but also have quite a but of experience modding Deltarune! I can mod essentially anything you want into the game. The only limitation is toby's awful code for some things. But for the most part, I can change whatever you want. Including:

  • Custom Battles/Bosses
  • Custom Sprites (Need to be provided to me)
  • Custom Items/Weapons
  • Custom Rooms (Sprites and Tilesets must be provided if applicable)

Q: Can you join my mod team?
A: Maybe. I already have a lot of things on my plate but if I really like your project I would be open to joining!

Q: If I commission you, can you do art?
A: Unfortunately no, I cannot do art. However if needing to pay for art cuts into your budget a sizable ammount I am open to reducing the price. I also have people I can contact for art if you don't know anyone.

Q: Will you "Work for exposure"
A: No. However, depending on the scale of your mod, I may consider doing it for free If I feel like it. However asking for it to be free or asking to work for exposure will prevent you from getting it for free.

Q: Will you do NSFW content?
A: Absolutely not. I am a minor. Anyone contanting me with 18+ requests will be blocked.
