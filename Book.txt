Chapter 1

I woke with a start. My hair was greasy with sweat, and my breathing was panicked. I looked around my room. The light of the sun filtered through the blinds of my window, casting it into a tranquil, yet ominous red. I got out of my bed and took a shower, as I sweat a lot in my sleep, especially on nights such as this. 

"What a nice day." I thought as I looked out my window at a cloudless sky.

"I hope it stays this way for the party tonight"

I had nothing to do, so I went downstairs to check my computer, and maybe work on a project or 2. As I walked downstairs I listened for the voice of my father. To my relief, I heard nothing. My dad often leaves without telling me to do god knows what. I played games on my computer and programmed to pass the time till tonight. Suddenly the grandfather clock chimed in the hallway. Dong. Dong. Dong. Dong. Dong. Dong. "Sh*t SH*T!" I yelled. I wasn't ready for the party at all. I had completely lost track of time. I sprinted upstairs and threw on my costume, A simple zombie mask and fake axe, then rushed back downstairs. As I ran out the door, I looked back at the grandfather clock. It was tall, made out of black ebony.
